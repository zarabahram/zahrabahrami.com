02 / HOW I WORK

Finding a way to think together.

I approach psychotherapy as a collaborative, reflective process. There is room for uncertainty, difference and the complexity of your own experience.

Beginning

Our first contact is an opportunity to ask questions and discuss what brings you to therapy. If we arrange an initial consultation, we can consider what you are looking for, whether psychodynamic psychotherapy seems appropriate, and the practical arrangements.

There is no obligation to continue after an initial conversation.

In our sessions

Rather than following a fixed script, we can attend to what you bring, including thoughts, feelings, memories, relationships and experiences that are difficult to describe.

I am attentive to the social, cultural and relational worlds in which our experiences take shape, without assuming that any single framework can explain a person's life.

Online psychotherapy

Sessions are intended to take place online with adults in the UK. A private space, reliable connection and suitable arrangements for confidentiality are important for online work.

Session length, frequency, fees, cancellations and availability will be discussed before any commitment. Please contact me for current details.
