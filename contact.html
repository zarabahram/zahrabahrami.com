Before getting in touch

Sessions are intended for adults aged 18 and over and offered online to people in the UK, subject to suitability and professional arrangements.

Emails are not monitored for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent mental health support in England, NHS 111 offers a mental health option; elsewhere in the UK, consult your local NHS urgent care guidance.
