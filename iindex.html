01 — About

About
me.

I am Zahra Bahrami, a psychologist and psychodynamic psychotherapist in training.

My work is informed by psychological, psychodynamic and intercultural perspectives. I am interested in how our experiences, relationships and histories can shape the ways we understand ourselves and the world around us.

I aim to offer a thoughtful, respectful and non-judgemental space in which you can explore what brings you to therapy at your own pace.

02 — Psychotherapy

What is
psychotherapy?

Psychodynamic psychotherapy is a way of working that explores the relationship between our present experiences and the feelings, relationships and patterns that have developed throughout our lives.

Therapy can provide an opportunity to become curious about thoughts and feelings, including experiences that may be difficult to recognise or express.

The work is collaborative and develops through the therapeutic relationship, with space to think together about what matters to you.
