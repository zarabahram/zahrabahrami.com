03 / ABOUT ME

Psychotherapy shaped by curiosity and context.

I am Zahra Bahrami. My interests lie in the relationship between our inner lives and the wider worlds—relational, cultural and historical—in which we live.

My approach

My work is informed by psychodynamic thinking and an interdisciplinary background in psychology, cognitive neuroscience and medical humanities. I am interested in what it means to be heard, how experiences acquire meaning, and what may remain difficult to express.

I value a therapeutic space where a person does not need to simplify themselves or fit their experience into a predetermined explanation.

Background and training

I hold an MSc in Clinical Psychology from Kharazmi University, Iran, as well as postgraduate degrees in Cognitive Neurosciences and Medical Humanities. I am undertaking psychodynamic psychotherapy training at the British Psychotherapy Foundation.

My clinical background includes mental health work in NHS settings and intercultural psychotherapy experience.

Professional memberships, registration details and training status should be verified and added here before the website is used to advertise independent clinical services.

Working across differences

I am particularly interested in how language, culture, migration, identity and belonging can shape emotional experience and the possibilities of being understood.

These are not assumptions about what matters to any individual; they are part of a commitment to listening openly to the specificity of each person's experience.
