A PLACE TO BEGIN

Sometimes the first step is simply having somewhere to speak freely.

Whether something specific has brought you here, or you are unsure what you need, therapy can begin with curiosity about your experience and what it might mean for you.

01 / THE APPROACH

Psychodynamic psychotherapy

Psychodynamic psychotherapy pays attention to how our histories, feelings and relationships shape the present, including patterns that may not yet be fully understood.

It is an opportunity to think together about what emerges, at a pace that allows the work to develop over time.

02 / WORKING TOGETHER

Attentive to the person, not just the difficulty.

My approach is grounded in listening, reflection and an interest in the different contexts of a person's life. Culture, identity, migration and belonging can be part of what shapes our experiences and how we speak about them.
